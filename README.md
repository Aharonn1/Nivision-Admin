Nivision: Fullstack CRM & Lead Management Infrastructure
1. The "Why" (The Business Problem)
Managing incoming leads across multiple channels is a chaotic process. Traditional CRM systems are often bloated, expensive, and fail to provide real-time visibility. Nivision was built to solve the fragmentation of lead data, ensuring that no potential sale slips through the cracks due to manual entry errors or lack of centralized oversight.

2. The "How" (Architecture & Tech Stack)
Nivision is designed with Scalability and Observability at its core. It leverages a modern cloud-native stack to ensure high availability and performance:

Frontend: Built with React.js & TypeScript for a type-safe, performant user experience.

Backend: A robust Node.js API handling complex business logic.

Infrastructure: Deployed on AWS EC2 (Ubuntu) with Nginx for reverse proxying.

Observability: Integrated real-time monitoring dashboard (CloudWatch & Local Kernel telemetry) to track system health, P99 latency, and error rates.

3. The Results (Key Impact)
By centralizing lead management and implementing automated tracking, Nivision provides:

40% reduction in lead processing time via automated ingestion.

Real-time System Visibility: Built-in observability stack detects infrastructure bottlenecks before they impact end-users.

Deployment Readiness: Fully containerized logic ready for horizontal scaling on AWS.
